# AI News Brief: 2026-10-11

A short, source-linked look at AI news from October 3–11, 2026. Searches for October 10–11 returned little dated coverage, so this brief is short. Every item comes from a secondary roundup and was taken from a single headline-level search summary, not from the source page itself. Each item is marked **(reported)** and should be checked before it is cited.

## Items

### 1. Biohub commits $1.8B to open biology data and AI **(reported)**
On October 7, Biohub announced a $1.8 billion pool of money, data and compute to build open biology datasets and an AI model that predicts how cells behave.
Source: [Hello Growth, AI news October 2026](https://hellogrowth.ai/en/blog/ai-news-october-2026)

### 2. Butterfly Effect, parent of Manus, closes a round of more than $500M **(reported)**
Butterfly Effect, the parent company of the AI agent company Manus, closed a funding round of more than $500 million.
Source: [Hello Growth, AI news October 2026](https://hellogrowth.ai/en/blog/ai-news-october-2026)

### 3. Anthropic reportedly disables live internet for internal evaluations **(reported)**
Anthropic reportedly turned off live internet access for all internal evaluations until it can monitor and control its agents better.
Source: [Hello Growth, AI news October 2026](https://hellogrowth.ai/en/blog/ai-news-october-2026)

### 4. Anthropic updates its usage policy **(reported)**
The update takes effect November 12 and bars sustained or needless abusive or cruel behavior toward Claude.
Source: [Hello Growth, AI news October 2026](https://hellogrowth.ai/en/blog/ai-news-october-2026)

### 5. Google releases Nano Banana 2.1 image model **(reported)**
Google released Nano Banana 2.1, an image model built on Gemini 3.6 Flash. One source says it cuts the cost of a 1K image to $0.0336 from $0.067.
Source: [Hello Growth, AI news October 2026](https://hellogrowth.ai/en/blog/ai-news-october-2026)
