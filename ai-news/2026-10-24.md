# AI News Brief: 2026-10-24

A short look at AI news from October 23, 2026, the most recent dated coverage the searches found. Every item comes from a daily digest, newsletter, or market report, not a primary announcement, so each is marked **(reported)**. Check each one before citing it.

## Items

### 1. Compromised LiteLLM release pulled from PyPI and Docker **(reported)**
Real Python reports that version 1.107.0 of LiteLLM, a popular AI gateway tool, was booby-trapped, and that the compromised copies were pulled from PyPI and Docker.
Source: [Real Python, AI News (Oct 17–23)](https://realpython.com/ai-news-weekly-2026-10-23/)

### 2. Storm-0940 reportedly used an agentic penetration-testing system in a ConnectWise compromise **(reported)**
According to The Neuron's October 23 digest, citing Microsoft, the threat actor Storm-0940 used the ARTEX agentic penetration-testing system during a ConnectWise compromise. The same digest reports that AI agents reached bank accounts in a separate incident.
Source: [The Neuron, Everything That Happened in AI Today, Friday, October 23](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-october-23-2026/)

### 3. Meta says over 10 million people have used Muse **(reported)**
Fortune reports that Mark Zuckerberg said more than 10 million people have used Meta's Muse.
Source: [Fortune, October 23, 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/10/23/another-horrible-day-markets-ai-oil-the-french/)

### 4. OpenAI reportedly forced to lower its valuation in a fundraising round **(reported)**
The same Fortune report says private equity investors pushed OpenAI to reduce the valuation it wanted in its latest fundraising round.
Source: [Fortune, October 23, 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/10/23/another-horrible-day-markets-ai-oil-the-french/)
