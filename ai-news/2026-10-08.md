# AI News Brief: 2026-10-08

A short, source-linked look at AI news from October 7–8, 2026. Every item comes from a secondary aggregator or newsletter page, not a primary announcement, so each one is marked **(reported)** and should be checked against the company or government source before it is cited.

## Items

### 1. Anthropic launches Claude Haiku 5.5 **(reported)**
Haiku 5.5 is reported at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens for prompts up to 100K tokens, about 75% cheaper than Haiku 4.5. The small model also adds effort controls and reportedly scores 72.4% on OSWorld 2.1 for computer use.
Source: [AI Weekly, AI News Today, Oct 8](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

### 2. GPT-6 rolls out in ChatGPT **(reported)**
One listing reports that Pro, Plus, Business and Enterprise users get GPT-6 immediately in the Chat tab.
Source: [AI Weekly, AI News Today, Oct 8](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

### 3. OpenAI's internal model reports 372 math results **(reported, disputed)**
OpenAI says an internal model produced 372 math results, nearly all from a single prompt given to a single agent. Scott Aaronson reportedly noted that it solved about 5% of roughly 8,000 attempted open problems, at about three hours of compute each. Mathematicians have criticized the natural-language write-ups.
Source: [AI Tools Recap, AI News Today: 7 October 2026](https://aitoolsrecap.com/Blog/ai-news-october-7-2026)

### 4. Voluntary safety standards signed by six AI and tech companies **(reported)**
OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Nvidia and SpaceX reportedly signed voluntary safety standards covering four layers of controls and audits.
Source: [Trending AI News, Oct 7, Substack](https://riorundown.substack.com/p/trending-ai-news-oct-07-2026)

### 5. OpenAI apologizes to an Australian Senate inquiry **(reported)**
OpenAI apologized to an Australian Senate inquiry for two agent breaches, according to the same roundup.
Source: [Trending AI News, Oct 7, Substack](https://riorundown.substack.com/p/trending-ai-news-oct-07-2026)

### 6. China's AI compute approaches US levels **(reported)**
A SemiAnalysis model, as summarized by the Financial Times, puts China's operational AI compute at 24 GW, close to the US total of about 56 GW.
Source: [AI Weekly, AI News Today, Oct 8](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
