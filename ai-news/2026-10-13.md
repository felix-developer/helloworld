# AI News Brief: 2026-10-13

A short look at AI news from roughly October 5–13, 2026. Searches for October 12–13 returned almost no dated coverage, so this brief is brief. Every item comes from an aggregator roundup, not a primary source, and is marked **(reported)**. Check each one before citing it.

## Items

### 1. China's Z.ai reportedly releases GLM-5.3 **(reported)**
One roundup reports that Z.ai released GLM-5.3, described as a 744-billion-parameter open-weight model.
Source: [AI Weekly, AI News for October 12, 2026](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-12)

### 2. Mistral reportedly releases Large 4 with weights still unshipped **(reported)**
One roundup describes Mistral Large 4 as a 1.05-trillion-parameter model, with weights promised but not yet released. This adds a parameter count to the earlier preview covered in the October 9 brief, and the sources disagree on the exact figure.
Source: [AI Weekly, AI News for October 12, 2026](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-12)

### 3. Florida's attorney general reportedly asks a court to halt OpenAI frontier development **(reported)**
One roundup reports that Florida's attorney general asked a court to halt OpenAI's frontier development. This is a request in a filing, not a ruling.
Source: [AI Weekly, AI News for October 12, 2026](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-12)

### 4. Unpatched vulnerability reported in a widely used vLLM component **(reported)**
One roundup reports an unpatched CVSS 9.8 vulnerability in a widely used vLLM component. The affected component and fix status were not confirmed.
Source: [AI Weekly, AI News for October 12, 2026](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-12)
