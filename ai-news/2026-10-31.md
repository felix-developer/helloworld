### 2. New York limits what AI chatbots can say to minors **(reported)**
The Neuron's October 30 digest reports that New York now limits what AI chatbots can say to minors. The law was signed on October 28, and the limits apply to every user unless the company has confirmed the user is an adult.
Source: [The Neuron, Everything That Happened in AI Today, Friday, October 30](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-october-30-2026/)

### 3. Anthropic files to list on Nasdaq as "ANT" **(reported)**
The same digest reports that Anthropic filed on October 26 to list on Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT." One roundup gives first-half 2026 revenue of $16.3 billion.
Source: [The Neuron, Everything That Happened in AI Today, Friday, October 30](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-october-30-2026/)
