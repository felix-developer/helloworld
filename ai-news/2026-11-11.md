## Items

### 1. Anthropic releases Fable 5.2 and limits Mythos 5.2 to vetted organizations **(reported)**
The Neuron's November 10 digest and other coverage that day report that Anthropic's Fable 5.2 model is available on the Claude Platform, AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure. Mythos 5.2 is limited to vetted cybersecurity and life-sciences organizations. One market newsletter says the new model is about 30% faster and 20% cheaper than 5.1.
Source: [The Neuron, Everything That Happened in AI Today, Tuesday, November 10](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-tuesday-november-10-2026/)
